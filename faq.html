Common Questions About RO Service

1. What services does Aro Home Service offer?

You can enquire about RO water purifier repair, routine servicing, filter and membrane replacement, installation and common problems such as leakage or low water flow. Service availability depends on your location and requirements.

2. Why is my RO water purifier not producing water?

Possible causes include low inlet water pressure, a blocked filter, a faulty pump, a closed valve or an electrical issue. Switch off the purifier if you notice leakage or an electrical fault and arrange a proper inspection.

3. Why is my RO purifier leaking water?

Leakage can occur because of loose connections, damaged tubing, worn seals or a cracked component. Turn off the water supply and unplug the unit if it is safe to do so, especially if water is near electrical parts. Contact a technician for inspection.

4. When should I replace my RO filters?

Replacement intervals depend on the filter type, water quality, usage and the purifier manufacturer's instructions. Ask for an inspection if water flow decreases or maintenance is overdue. Do not rely on a single replacement schedule for every purifier.

5. What does an RO membrane do?

An RO membrane separates many dissolved substances from water through a reverse-osmosis process. Its performance depends on water conditions, usage and maintenance. A technician can check whether the membrane needs cleaning or replacement.

6. Why is my purifier dispensing water slowly?

Slow dispensing may be caused by a clogged filter, low inlet pressure, an issue with the pump or an empty or pressurised storage tank problem. The cause should be checked before replacing any parts.
