RO Problems We Can Help You Enquire About

If your purifier is not working as expected, describe the symptoms when contacting us. The exact fault should be diagnosed before replacing any component.

RO purifier not producing water

No water output can be associated with a water supply issue, clogged filters, pump problems or a faulty valve. Basic checks and professional inspection can help identify the cause.

Slow water flow

Slow output may be related to the inlet supply, filters, membrane, pump or storage tank system. The cause depends on the purifier model and operating conditions.

Water leakage

Leakage may occur around tubing, connectors, filter housings or the storage tank. Stop using a leaking purifier and arrange an inspection. Keep away from wet electrical components.

Filter and membrane servicing

Filter and membrane maintenance depends on the model, incoming water quality and usage. A service check can help determine whether replacement is required.
