RO Repair Problems in Sakri

If your water purifier is not working as expected, share its symptoms and model details when contacting us. An inspection can help identify the cause before any parts are replaced.

RO purifier not producing water

A lack of water output may be related to the incoming water supply, filters, pump, valves or other components. The actual cause depends on your purifier.

Slow water flow

Slow flow may occur because of clogged filters, low inlet pressure, membrane performance or a storage tank issue. A technician can assess which component needs attention.

Water leakage

Leaks can occur around tubes, fittings, filter housings or the storage tank. Stop using a leaking purifier and keep away from wet electrical components. Arrange an inspection before using it again.

RO filter servicing

Filter replacement depends on the purifier model, water quality and usage. Follow the manufacturer's maintenance schedule and seek an inspection if performance changes.
