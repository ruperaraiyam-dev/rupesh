RO Repair & Service FAQs

What services can I enquire about?

You can enquire about RO repair, servicing, installation, filter replacement, membrane replacement and common purifier problems. Confirm availability before arranging a visit.

How much does RO repair cost?

Costs depend on the problem, purifier model, replacement parts and visit charges. Contact us for an estimate before authorising work.

When should I replace an RO filter?

Replacement timing varies by filter type, water quality, usage and manufacturer recommendations. Have the purifier checked if water flow or quality changes unexpectedly.
